## 5. The First 30 Days: One Offer

**Primary offer: the devotional sold direct at $13.95, with the first month of the private
community included.**

Rationale: 2,000 copies are already printed and sitting in inventory, the ask is natural in
a room she is already teaching nightly, there is no price objection at $13.95, and every
buyer becomes three assets at once (email subscriber, community member, card on file).
That buyer list is what fills the $97 workshop in month 2.

Everything else stays quiet in month 1: the affiliate course, the insurance policies, the
workshop. Speaking runs in parallel only because the outreach agent does not compete for
the audience's attention.

**The journey:** nightly live, one pinned link, landing page, checkout, instant delivery
plus community invite, 5-email welcome sequence in her voice, day-21 invitation to the
paid workshop.

**Community mechanic, as contracted:** a book purchase includes the first month of community
access. After that month, continued access is a $19 per month recurring membership through
Stripe, cancellable any time. "Members with access" and "recurring paying members" are
counted and reported separately so that book-purchase access is never reported as recurring
revenue.
