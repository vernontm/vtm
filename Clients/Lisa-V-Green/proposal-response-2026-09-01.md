**14. If the offer is not converting in the first 30 days**

We diagnose by number, not by feel. The funnel has four measurable steps, and only one of them is usually broken:

1. **Are people seeing it?** Views and live attendance. If this is the problem, the fix is content and frequency.
2. **Are they clicking?** Link clicks divided by views. If views are fine but clicks are not, the problem is the call to action and where it sits in the live.
3. **Are they landing and staying?** Landing page visits and time on page. If they click and bounce, the page is the problem, headline and promise get rewritten.
4. **Are they buying?** Checkout starts and completions. If they land and do not buy, the offer or the price or the trust is the problem, and if they start checkout and do not finish, it is a mechanical problem we fix same day.

**The cadence:** we check these numbers weekly, not at day 30. Day 14 is a formal check-in with a written read. If two of those four steps are underperforming at day 14, we change one variable at a time, starting with the cheapest to change (the call to action, then the page, then the offer, then the price). We do not change all four at once, because then we learn nothing.

**And if the offer itself is wrong**, we say so plainly and move to the next one rather than defending a decision. That is the point of picking one offer per 30 days.
