Common questions

Do you work with businesses outside Katy?

Yes. Katy is home base and where most in-person work happens, including Cypress, Cinco Ranch, Fulshear and the rest of greater Houston. Builds themselves are remote-friendly, so clients outside Texas are welcome too.

What does AI automation actually mean for a small business?

In practice it means the repetitive parts of the business stop needing a person. Leads get captured and followed up automatically, quotes and invoices generate themselves, content gets drafted and scheduled, and the owner sees one dashboard instead of six tabs. It is plumbing between the tools you already pay for, plus AI where a human would otherwise retype things.

Can you build a custom CRM instead of selling me a subscription?

Yes, and it is the most common request. A custom CRM built for one business is usually cheaper over two years than a per-seat subscription, and it matches how the business actually works instead of forcing a rewrite of the process. We also publish a free guide that walks through building one yourself.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so pricing is quoted per project after a free call rather than listed as a package price. The call is 15 minutes and ends with a straight answer about what the build involves and roughly what it costs, whether or not you hire us.

Do you run local events?

Yes. We run AI workshops and networking meetups for business owners in the Katy and Cypress area, and we built OnlyLocals.ai to list local events and connect people in the area.
